Rachel - Privacy

Last updated 8 October 2026

Rachel is my own private assistant app. It is not a public service and nobody can sign up to it; it is used only by me and people I personally give access to.

What Rachel accesses

Rachel runs on my own Google account. With my permission it reads and writes my own Google Sheets, Docs, Drive files and Gmail so it can keep my bookings, reminders and notes organised.

Where data is kept

Everything stays in my own Google account. Rachel does not sell, share or advertise with any data. Some text is sent to Google's Gemini service to help read and draft messages, under Google's terms.

Phone notifications

If pop-ups are turned on, the phone's notification token is stored so Rachel can send reminders to that phone. Turning pop-ups off or removing the phone in Settings deletes it.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from your Google Account under Security → Third-party connections.
